How we describe this work

Positioning, depending on the room

The central statement

XL Marketing Services is a strategic marketing and growth consultancy that helps organisations make better decisions about markets, customers, positioning and growth. Through its Decision-Led Growth approach, XL combines market intelligence, behavioural understanding and commercial strategy to help businesses create stronger demand, better customer outcomes and sustainable value.

In commercial terms

We help organisations make better strategic marketing and growth decisions by understanding how people evaluate, choose, buy, adopt and remain loyal.

For leadership & board-level conversations

We help leadership teams make consequential growth decisions with greater confidence by improving the quality of the evidence, questions and assumptions informing them.

Suitable for executive presentations, research and intelligence services, board-level discussions, market-entry engagements, and high-consequence strategic projects.

In practical, customer-first language

We help businesses grow by making it easier for customers to understand their options, recognise meaningful value and make decisions that help them progress.

About XL Marketing Services

XL Marketing Services is a strategic marketing and growth consultancy helping founders, CEOs, CMOs and marketing leaders improve the evidence, choices and systems behind commercial action.

We work across brand positioning, go-to-market and product marketing, B2B growth and account-based marketing, marketing-sales alignment, AI search visibility, and fractional CMO advisory. Our role is to help organisations decide what deserves attention, translate that decision into coherent execution and learn from the market without losing strategic consistency.

Core promise

Better marketing decisions that create credible positioning, customer value and sustainable commercial progress.

Decisions That Compound.

Where growth stalls

Marketing can be busy while the decision remains unresolved

A new campaign, technology platform or content format can create activity without resolving the question underneath it. The audience may be unclear. The proposition may not be credible. Marketing and sales may be working from different definitions of value.

XL helps leadership teams locate the decision that is shaping the outcome, examine the evidence and assumptions around it, and design the next appropriate action — keeping marketing connected to customer value and commercial priorities while making operational and customer-experience dependencies visible.

What we believe

Decisions are only as good as the evidence behind them

The quality of marketing decisions cannot consistently exceed the quality of the information, questions and assumptions from which they are made.

Marketing should help organisations understand markets and people more accurately, make credible choices, communicate value clearly and create conditions in which customers can decide with confidence. It should also respect customer agency — behavioural science can reduce unnecessary friction and improve comprehension; it should not disguise manipulation as optimisation.

How XL works

Marketing Decision-Led Growth

Our approach connects four recurring disciplines. It is an adaptive operating cycle rather than a rigid sequence imposed on every engagement.

  • Understand: clarify the market, customer, organisational conditions and quality of available evidence.
  • Decide: identify the choice that matters, the trade-offs involved and what evidence is sufficient to proceed.
  • Design: translate the decision into positioning, systems, experiences, communications or coordinated activity.
  • Learn: examine market response, commercial movement, customer experience and new evidence to improve the next decision.

The XL Crosspoint supports the initial diagnostic conversation by locating where evidence, decisions, execution and learning intersect — or where they have become disconnected. The diagnostic guides judgement; it does not replace it.

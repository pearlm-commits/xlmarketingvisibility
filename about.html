About XL

Strategic marketing intelligence, connected to commercial action

XL Marketing Services helps organisations make better decisions about markets, customers, positioning and growth—and turn those decisions into coordinated marketing and business-development action.

We combine strategic marketing leadership, market and buyer intelligence, account-based marketing, partnership development and structured commercial engagement. This allows us to support more than visibility: we help clients identify the right organisations, reach the right decision-makers and create credible opportunities for commercial conversations.

XL is led by Pearl M and Tameka Hendrikz, whose experience brings together two disciplines that are too often separated: marketing strategy and business development.

Pearl M
Chief Marketing Officer and Strategic Marketing Lead

Pearl is a fractional CMO and brand strategist with more than 25 years of experience across telecommunications, fintech, enterprise technology, infrastructure, luxury services and emerging technology.

Her leadership experience includes Vodacom, PayU, Dimension Data/NTT, Openserve, Altron and Alcatel-Lucent, with responsibility spanning South Africa, 54 African markets and the Middle East.

Pearl's work encompasses market intelligence, brand positioning, product marketing, go-to-market strategy, marketing transformation and executive communications. She served as marketing lead on Vodacom's South African M-Pesa launch project team and subsequently led B2B marketing for Vodacom Business across 54 African markets, where marketing-generated pipeline contributed to 30% gross-profit growth.

At XL, Pearl leads the strategic architecture: defining the market opportunity, clarifying the proposition, understanding the customer decision and creating the marketing system required to build demand.

Tameka Hendrikz
Head of Business Development and Partnerships

Tameka brings the commercial and relationship-building discipline that turns strategy into market access.

As Head of Partnerships at Xplora Luxe, she built the company's partner network from zero to more than 100 partners, creating the relationships required to support an international luxury travel and concierge proposition.

She also led Xplora Luxe's entry into Eureka GCC, a highly competitive entrepreneurial incubator competition. The business was selected among the top 33 start-ups from approximately 500 submissions—placing it within the top 6.6% of applicants.

Tameka applies a structured business-development approach to target-account identification, partnership development, account-based marketing, prospect research, personalised outreach, multi-touch follow-up, executive delegate acquisition and pipeline progression.

Her sales leadership enables XL to assess marketing from the receiving end of the pipeline: whether the audience is properly qualified, whether the proposition supports a commercial conversation and whether follow-up can convert interest into opportunity.

Problem recognition

When activity is moving faster than clarity

Marketing problems rarely begin with a shortage of content, campaigns or ideas. They begin earlier: with incomplete market intelligence, indistinct positioning, uncertain priorities or assumptions about customers that have never been properly tested.

The result is familiar. Teams remain busy, channels multiply and budgets are spent, but leadership still cannot confidently explain what is creating demand, what should change or where the next investment should go.

XL helps resolve that uncertainty before more activity is added.

How XL works

A more disciplined way to create growth

Decisions are only as strong as the evidence and inquiry fueling them. For better commercial impact, we start by upgrading the quality of your information and the questions you ask.

Our Decision-Led Growth approach anchors every marketing output in the business decisions that need to improve:

  • What the organisation needs to decide, financially, strategically and positionally
  • What the market and customer evidence reveals
  • Which assumptions need to be tested
  • What customers need to understand, believe or do
  • Where friction is preventing progress
  • How success should be measured

The result is not simply another strategy document; it is a clearer basis for action, execution and learning.

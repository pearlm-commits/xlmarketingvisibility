XL's Growth Methodology

Find what needs to change for people and the business to move forward.

Marketing Decision-Led Growth is XL's evidence-led method for improving the marketing decisions, conditions and experiences that shape progress.

It combines credible evidence with human understanding to locate the XL Crosspoint: the productive point at which human progress, business ambition, credible brand contribution and the conditions for movement create mutual value.

Our governing belief

People seek progress. Businesses seek growth. Marketing creates value when it connects the two.

People are already trying to solve problems, make better decisions and move their lives or organisations forward. A brand earns a role by helping them achieve that progress — and earns sustainable growth by creating value people can recognise, trust and experience.

The question we ask

What needs to change for people and the business to move forward?

How it works

A more disciplined way to create growth

Decisions are only as strong as the evidence and inquiry fueling them. For better commercial impact, we start by upgrading the quality of your information and the questions you ask.

Our Marketing Decision-Led Growth approach begins with the progress people are already pursuing, then identifies the role the brand can legitimately earn:

  • What progress people are already trying to make
  • What they currently think, feel and do in context
  • What makes a better decision difficult
  • What information, proof, access or experience would help
  • What useful role the brand can legitimately earn
  • How that role can create value for people and the business
  • What evidence will tell us whether either is progressing

The result is not simply another strategy document; it is a clearer basis for action, execution and learning.

What governs the work

The quality of decisions cannot consistently exceed the quality of the information, questions and assumptions from which they are made.

How we build in accountability

Accountability follows authority. XL defines the contribution marketing is expected to make, identifies the operational and customer-experience dependencies affecting the outcome, and accepts accountability for the decisions, work and measures within its control and influence.

  • We distinguish evidence from assumptions and interpretation.
  • We do not use communication to disguise an unsuitable proposition or experience.
  • We do not treat human limitations as vulnerabilities to exploit.
  • We design for informed, voluntary and accessible decisions.
  • We identify what marketing can influence and what depends on product, pricing, sales, operations or market conditions.
  • We measure customer value and commercial progress — not activity alone.

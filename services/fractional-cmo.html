Frequently asked

Questions worth answering directly

What is a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing executive who works with a business on a part-time or flexible basis, providing strategic leadership, direction and accountability without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

When does a business need a fractional CMO rather than a full-time hire?

A fractional CMO tends to suit businesses that need senior marketing judgement but not yet a full-time executive seat — during a growth stage, a leadership gap, a specific strategic decision, or before the business is ready to commit to a permanent hire.

How many days a week does a fractional CMO typically work?

Fractional arrangements vary by business need, typically ranging from one day a week to a few days a month, structured around the specific decisions and oversight the business requires rather than a fixed default.

Can a fractional CMO manage an existing marketing team?

Yes. A fractional CMO can provide leadership, direction and accountability to an existing marketing team, in addition to or instead of building strategy from scratch.

What is the difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing consultant?

A marketing consultant typically advises on a defined project or question. A fractional CMO holds ongoing leadership responsibility and accountability for marketing outcomes, similar to an executive role, on a part-time basis.
